# Research playback design
Warm off-white canvas (#f5f3ee), dark ink (#20332e), green baize (#21634f), purple front-core identifier (#bc83ff on baize, #7343a1 on paper). Native system CJK sans for local Mac playback; tabular numeric text. Two equal panels share projection, physical scale and simulation time. Full-table row plus early rack detail row. No invented decorative animation: every moving ball follows saved native state. Scientific playback intentionally overrides marketing scene decoration. Canvas1440×1000, responsive player outside fixed composition. User controls never change trajectories. Pocket shapes simplified; no 3D spin rendering.
