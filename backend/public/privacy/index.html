二、权限

  • 相册读取：仅在您选择头像、图片或训练视频时请求。
  • 相册写入：仅在您主动保存训练分享卡时请求。
  • 相机：仅在您主动拍摄训练视频时请求。
  • 通知：仅在您开启训练提醒时请求，可随时在系统设置中关闭。

拒绝非必要权限不会影响与该权限无关的功能。

QiuJi Privacy Policy

Effective: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · Data controller: {{OPERATOR_NAME}}

QiuJi does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or track users across apps or websites.

Data we process

When you sign in, we process your account identifier and optional name or email supplied by Apple. If you choose to provide them, we process your display name, avatar, billiards preferences, skill level, years of experience, weekly goal, training sessions, dates and durations, scores, notes, angle-test results, and tool interaction dates and durations. Purchase entitlement is obtained through Apple; we do not receive full payment credentials.

Device permissions

Photo-library access is requested only when you choose media; write access is used when you save a share card; camera access is used when you record training media; notifications are requested only when you enable reminders. Other photos are not uploaded unless you explicitly select and confirm one as your avatar.

Use, processors, and sharing

We use data to authenticate accounts, sync training records, provide history and statistics, restore purchases, support users, secure the service, and improve product functionality. Infrastructure is provided by {{INFRASTRUCTURE_PROVIDER}} in {{DATA_REGION}}. Apple processes data needed for Sign in with Apple, the App Store, and StoreKit under its own policies. We do not disclose personal data to other controllers unless required by law or with separate consent.

Retention and security

We retain account data only while needed to provide the service. Account deletion removes the account, cloud training records, angle tests, and avatar from the primary database. Restricted security backups are overwritten or deleted within {{BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS}} days unless law requires otherwise. We use access controls, encrypted transport, credential isolation, and least-privilege measures. Account and training cloud data are stored in {{DATA_REGION}} and are not intentionally transferred abroad by us.
