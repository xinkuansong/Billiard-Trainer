二、权限

  • 选择照片：设置头像或导入球桌照片时，iOS 系统选择器只向 CuePhys 提供您选中的图片；您可以取消选择。
  • 保存分享卡：点击“保存相册”时，CuePhys 会请求向相册添加照片的权限。拒绝后仍可记录训练。
  • 球桌照片可以先用手机相机拍好，再从系统照片选择器导入。CuePhys 当前不调用设备相机。
  • 通知：保存并开启训练提醒，或手动开始组间休息时，CuePhys 会在首次需要时请求通知权限。自动开始休息不弹出授权框；拒绝后仍可训练和查看倒计时。

拒绝非必要权限不会影响与该权限无关的功能。

三、处理依据、共享与委托处理

我们基于您的同意、履行服务协议所必需以及适用法律允许的其他依据处理信息。账号和训练数据由 {{INFRASTRUCTURE_PROVIDER}} 在 {{DATA_REGION}} 提供基础设施处理。我们只向实现服务所必要的受托方提供最少信息，并要求其采取不低于本政策的保护措施。

您选择微信登录或绑定时，App 会通过腾讯微信 OpenSDK 检查微信是否已安装并发起授权。您同意后，我们的服务器向微信交换并核验一次性授权码，保存该应用对应的微信标识。CuePhys 服务端不调用微信用户资料接口，也不读取或保存您的微信昵称、头像。根据腾讯的说明，iOS SDK 可在设备本地保存用于适配的设备型号，并使用系统剪切板在应用之间传递授权相关信息。详见微信 Open SDK 个人信息处理规则。

Apple 分别处理 Sign in with Apple、App Store 和 StoreKit 交易所需信息，其处理受 Apple 自身条款和隐私政策约束。除上述必要服务、依法履行义务或获得您的另行同意外，我们不会向其他个人信息处理者提供、公开或出售您的信息。

四、保存、安全与跨境

账号数据在账号存续及提供服务所必需的最短期限内保存。您注销账号后，主业务数据库中的账号、训练记录、角度测试、自定义球形及其同步记录和头像将删除。定期业务备份按 {{BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS}} 天滚动清理。如果后续备份中断，最近一份已验证的备份可能保存更久。历史手工备份单独管理。备份仅用于安全恢复，访问受到限制。恢复服务前，我们会根据注销记录再次删除已注销账号的数据。另行保存的注销记录含内部账号标识、注销阶段、时间和校验信息，用于从备份恢复时继续执行删除。法律要求另行保存的除外。

我们采取访问控制、传输加密、凭证隔离和最小权限等措施。当前账号与训练云数据存储在 {{DATA_REGION}}，不会主动向境外提供；Apple 自有服务的数据流以 Apple 的说明为准。如未来发生处理目的、接收方或跨境情况变化，我们会依法更新告知并在需要时另行取得同意。

定期业务备份以加密形式另存于我们管理的备份设备；服务器中转包按两天、旧注销证明包按七天滚动清理，最近有效副本和原始注销记录另行保留以防止恢复后数据重新出现。上述滚动清理期限不是全部历史副本或注销记录的统一删除期限。

CuePhys Privacy Policy

Effective: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · Data controller: {{OPERATOR_NAME}}

CuePhys does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or track users across apps or websites.

Data we process

If you choose WeChat sign-in or link WeChat in account settings, Tencent’s WeChat OpenSDK checks whether WeChat is installed and opens its authorization screen. After you authorize, our server exchanges the one-time code with WeChat and stores the app-specific WeChat identifier (OpenID) with the application identifier to identify your CuePhys account. Our server does not call the WeChat profile endpoint or retrieve or store your WeChat nickname or avatar. Tencent states that its iOS SDK can retain the device model locally for compatibility and use the system pasteboard to communicate with WeChat. See the WeChat Open SDK privacy rules.

When you sign in, we process your account identifier and optional name or email supplied by Apple. If you choose to provide them, we process your display name, avatar, optional gender and date of birth, billiards preferences, skill level, years of experience, weekly goal, training sessions, dates and durations, scores, notes, angle-test results, and tool interaction dates and durations. When you enable account sync, saved custom formations include their names, ball positions, ball identities and guide lines. Guest formations require separate merge consent; unsaved drafts and active practice state are not uploaded. When purchasing, we provide Apple with an app-specific account token and match it against the verified transaction when unlocking or restoring membership; we do not receive full payment credentials.

Guest training records and guest avatars are stored locally. For signed-in users, the account sync setting controls training records, results and saved formations. Declining a guest merge keeps those records on this device. Tool-use records follow the same account sync setting. Turning sync off keeps new records on this device and preserves existing cloud copies. Turning it back on syncs the account’s pending records. Saving account profile changes or an avatar sends those changes to the CuePhys server. When you create a formation from a photo, you mark the ball positions on your device. Only the saved formation data is included in sync.

Device permissions

The iOS photo picker gives CuePhys only the image you select for an avatar or table layout. You can cancel the picker. CuePhys does not access the device camera. Saving a training share card requests add-only photo access. Enabling a training reminder or manually starting a rest can request notification permission. An automatic rest does not prompt. You can continue training if you decline either permission. Other photos are not uploaded unless you explicitly select and confirm one as your avatar.

Use, processors, and sharing

We use data to authenticate accounts, sync training records, provide history and statistics, restore purchases, support users, secure the service, and improve product functionality. Infrastructure is provided by {{INFRASTRUCTURE_PROVIDER}} in {{DATA_REGION}}. Apple processes data needed for Sign in with Apple, the App Store, and StoreKit under its own policies. We do not disclose personal data to other controllers unless required by law or with separate consent.

Retention and security

We retain account data only while needed to provide the service. Account deletion removes the account, cloud training records, angle tests, custom formations and their sync history, and avatar from the primary database. We rotate scheduled business backups on a {{BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS}}-day schedule. The latest verified backup may be kept longer if subsequent backups are interrupted. Older manual backups are managed separately. Access to backups is restricted to recovery purposes. Before restoring service, we use deletion records to remove data for deleted accounts again. We use access controls, encrypted transport, credential isolation, and least-privilege measures. Account and training cloud data are stored in {{DATA_REGION}} and are not intentionally transferred abroad by us.

A separate deletion record retains the internal account identifier, deletion stage, time and integrity information so a backup restore can reapply the deletion.

Encrypted scheduled business backups are also stored on a backup device we manage. Server transfer packages rotate after two days and older deletion-proof packages after seven days; the latest valid copies and original deletion records are retained separately to prevent deleted data from reappearing after recovery. The scheduled business-backup window is not a blanket deletion deadline for all historical copies or deletion records.

Your choices

You can edit profile data or delete your account in the app. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to request access, a copy, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent. Users under 14 can practise locally as guests. Answering the age-band survey is optional. An eligible response is kept on the device until successful sign-in, then used for aggregate audience statistics. Skipped responses and under-14 age bands are not sent. During sign-in the service receives a confirmation of age 14 or above and includes it in the session credentials. Sign-in, cloud sync and purchases are unavailable to users under 14. Guardians may use the contact address above for requests concerning existing data.
