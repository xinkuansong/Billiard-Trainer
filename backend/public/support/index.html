登录遇到问题

要继续本地练习，可在登录页选择“暂不登录，匿名使用”。如果要找回已有账号的训练记录，请告诉我们你使用的登录方式和页面上的错误提示。

恢复永久会员

在 App Store 使用购买时的 Apple 账号，然后登录购买时使用的 CuePhys 账号，在会员页点“恢复购买”。如果权益仍未显示，请把恢复结果和 App 版本发给我们。

账号和个人数据

要注销账号，请打开“我的”中的设置，点“注销账号”，阅读提示后确认。要查阅、更正或删除个人数据，也可以来信说明要处理的内容。详情见隐私政策。

Sign-in help

You can continue local practice as a guest from the sign-in screen. For help accessing an existing account’s training history, email us with the sign-in method you used and the error shown in the app.

Restore a lifetime purchase

Use the Apple Account you used for the purchase in the App Store. Sign in to the CuePhys account used for the purchase and choose Restore Purchases on the membership screen. If access is still missing, email us the restore result and your app version.

Account and personal data

To delete your account, open Settings from your profile, choose the account deletion option and review the confirmation. You can also email us to request access, correction or deletion of personal data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
